01 · How it works

Three steps from a lead to a report

1

Open a case

Give it a number, a classification, the primary target and the legal basis. The classification band follows you onto every page.

2

Collect and connect

More than 50 identifier types, subject photos, locations and evidence. Drag to link them and grade each source with an Admiralty code.

3

Write the report

Cover, subject profiles, network diagram, timeline and exhibits — dark or light, with optional redaction and watermark.

02 · Screens

The whole investigation in one window

Several main and additional photos per person — drag & drop or paste with Ctrl+V. A SHA-256 hash of every original is kept.

03 · Features

What is on the desk

Link network

Drag-to-connect nodes; confirmed, probable and doubtful links; shortest path and centrality.

Subject profiles

Role, threat level and description, with several main and additional photos per person.

50+ identifiers

Friends, family, work and education; 25+ platforms from Instagram to GitHub; breach records.

Map & sketch

OpenStreetMap with no key, or Google Maps. Visits, sightings, radius and density.

Timeline

Events, visits, sightings and exhibits merged into one month-by-month timeline.

Evidence vault

SHA-256 integrity hash, re-verification and chain of custody. Every action is logged.

PDF & HTML report

Dark or light theme, page numbers, classification band, redaction and watermark.

Encrypted files

Lock a case with AES-256-GCM. The browser autosave copy is encrypted too.

04 · Report

A report you can hand over

  • Cover with the target photo, case header and file fingerprint
  • A photo profile for every subject, with linked identifiers
  • Network diagram, location sketch and visual timeline
  • Classification band and “Page X / Y” on every page
  • With redaction on, sensitive values never reach the output
  • English or Turkish, following the interface language

06 · Privacy

No server, no account, no tracking

Data stays in the browser

The app runs entirely on your device. Cases live in this browser; downloading and storing them is up to you.

No sign-up, no login

No e-mail, no password, no account. Open the page and start a case.

Responsible use

OSINT Case is built for authorised investigations and research. Process personal data only with a lawful basis and purpose.
