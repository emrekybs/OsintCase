02 / Screens

A whole investigation in one window

Several main and additional photos per person — drag & drop or paste with Ctrl+V. A SHA-256 hash of every original is kept.

03 / Features

Everything an investigation desk needs

Link network

Drag-to-connect nodes, confirmed / probable / doubtful links, shortest path and centrality.

Subject profiles

Role, threat level and description. Several main and additional photos, each with a SHA-256 hash.

50+ identifiers

Acquaintances, family, employment, education; 25+ platforms from Instagram to GitHub, WhatsApp to Telegram channels; breach records.

Map & sketch

OpenStreetMap with no key, or Google Maps. Visits, sightings, radius and density.

Timeline

Events, location visits, sightings and exhibits merged into one month-by-month timeline.

Evidence vault

SHA-256 integrity hash, re-verification and chain of custody. Every action goes into the audit log.

PDF & HTML report

Dark or light theme, page numbers, classification band. Redaction for sensitive fields, watermark for leak tracing.

Encrypted files

Protect a case with AES-256-GCM. The autosave copy in the browser is encrypted too.

06 / Privacy

No server. No account. No tracking.

Data stays in the browser

The app runs entirely on your device. Cases live in this browser; downloading and storing them is up to you.

No sign-up, no login

No e-mail, no password, no account. Open the page and start a case.

Responsible use

OSINT Case is built for authorised investigations and research. Process personal data only with a lawful basis and purpose.
